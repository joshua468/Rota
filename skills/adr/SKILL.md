# ADR

Create `docs/decisions/NNNN-short-title.md` (next number in sequence):

```
# NNNN. Title
Date: YYYY-MM-DD · Status: accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Problem
What forced a decision? Constraints.

## Options considered
1. A: pros / cons
2. B: pros / cons

## Decision
What we chose, in one sentence.

## Consequences
What gets easier, what gets harder, what we'd revisit and when.
```
Keep it under one page, specific, and true. Link it from the relevant doc.
